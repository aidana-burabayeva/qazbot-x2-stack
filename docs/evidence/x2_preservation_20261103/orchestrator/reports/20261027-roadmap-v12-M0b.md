## 3. Stopping, overshoot, tolerances, caps
Real robot evidence (all odometry-based, no tape p95):
- G3 (22.10, ~1.2 m goal): commands 0.30→0.12 gave actual 0.39–0.49 m/s. Command integral 0.62 m vs real 1.155 m (LIO), 1.063 m (leg odom), 1.18 m (AMCL), i.e. ×1.6–1.9. Coast 0.14 m over 1.76 s after the last fwd>0. Start lag 0.28–0.47 s. Cross-track ~0.13 m after a yaw kick at gait start. Final position 0.19 m past the goal, yaw error 0.59 rad.
- Cycle 2 (22.10): 0.5 m goal SUCCEEDED, with physical overshoot 0.14 m. SUCCEEDED fires when the pose passes inside 0.10 m; the robot then coasts.
- Rotation: right turns are faster (0.92 vs 0.29 rad/s at |wz|=0.6). Coast after zero +4.5° (trial01); trial03 swung back ~21°. Response depends on state.
- Twin only (SIM, MC v1.0.0, not .72): 0.12 → 0.440 m/s, coast 0.23 m; 0.20 → 0.449, coast 0.16–0.29 m; 0.30 → 0.39–0.42, coast 0.23–0.25 m. So "cmd 0.12 → 0.44 m/s" is a SIM figure, consistent with G3 on the real robot but not measured by a step on .72.
- So the effective walking speed is ~0.4–0.5 m/s whatever the command between 0.12 and 0.3. Commanded stopping (smoother decel 0.6: 0.3→0 in 0.5 s) does not bound the physical stop. Expect a physical stop of ~0.15–0.30 m after the last forward command.

Tolerances: xy 0.10 / yaw 0.10 with stateful=false (live). For straight goals I expect "SUCCEEDED and then coasting past by 0.1–0.25 m". If the robot misses the 0.10 m circle laterally (cross-track 0.13 m in G3), it overshoots and the goal ends up behind it. RPP then rotates about 180° at 0.6 and walks back, which risks oscillation until the progress checker or the 30 s alignment watch aborts.

Safe caps for M1 (recommendation; changing them is a launch-arg decision, not done here):
- Keep max_linear 0.3 and max_angular 0.6. Do NOT lower min_approach below 0.12: below ~0.1 the robot may not step, and lowering the command does not lower the real speed.
- Plan for a physical speed of 0.5 m/s and a stopping envelope of ≥0.4 m beyond the goal. Free space: ≥2.0 m ahead of the start along the path, and ≥1.0 m either side.
- M1 xy_tol: prefer 0.20 with stateful:=true (the 22.10 robot-proven setting; both are existing launch args xy_tol / goal_checker_stateful). This avoids rotate-at-goal hunting caused by the 0.107 m IMU-footprint lever (base_footprint moves ~0.1 m during in-place rotation, native-odom design §3.3). Decision: user.
