## Change (x2_motion_adapter.py, ws + qazbot-x2-stack mirror; backups *.bak_20261029_pre_p20)
- `YawRateEstimator`: yaw rate from `/qazbot_native/odom` pose yaw. It differentiates with the header-stamp dt and applies a 1st-order LPF (tau 0.25 s). A gap over 0.5 s restarts it.
  - The odom twist is NOT used: a live sample while standing had angular.z = 1.78 rad/s and linear.y = 0.30.
- `YawRateLoop`, active only for pure rotation: |vx| < 0.02, vy = 0, |wz| > 0.05, and a measurement fresher than 0.3 s.
  - Output: u = sign(w) * clamp(u_ff(|w|) + I, u_min_dir, u_max).
  - u_ff comes from the p19 static from-stand curve: CCW (0.45→0.6, 1.25→0.8, 1.87→1.0); CW (0.27→0.4, 0.54→0.6, extrapolated 1.2→0.8, 1.8→1.0).
  - I = ki * (target − measured) with ki 0.4. Anti-windup is conditional integration plus a clamp.
  - There is no integration in the first 0.6 s after entry (measured MC dead time ~0.5 s).
  - u is rate-limited to 1.5/s; the first sample is not limited.
  - Overspeed: when measured > max(1.3 * target, target + 0.25), u bleeds toward u_min at 1.5/s. The +0.25 margin is there because filtered gait ripple is about ±0.2 rad/s.
  - Reset on sign change, on leaving pure rotation, on a stale measurement (then the command passes through unchanged), and on every non-driving tick (`_drain`).
  - So zero or stale input gives zero output immediately: no integral survives.
  - The result is still saturated to the live MC angular bounds. vx is passed through.
- Safety path unchanged: latches, gates, zero bursts, trust/T1 pause, takeover and registration logic are untouched. The loop runs only inside the existing "motion" branch.
- ROS params: `yaw_loop` (default true; false = exact pre-P20 behaviour), `yaw_u_min_ccw` 0.6, `yaw_u_min_cw` 0.45, `yaw_u_max` 1.0, `yaw_ki` 0.4, `odom_topic`.
- Logging: one INFO line per second while the loop is active: `yaw_loop target= meas= u= I=`. New counters are `yaw_loop` and `yaw_meas_stale`.
- Checks:
  - py_compile OK.
  - Dry-run smoke start in a separate namespace (/p20_dry, live=false) came up cleanly, printed its params and exited on SIGTERM.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| CCW stand | 3.1 s | 8.2° | −2.5° | 1 |

Note: an RPP-style emulator with a ±5° band and no slowdown limit-cycles. The coast after a zero command (MC dead time) is about 10°, which exceeds that band. In Nav2 the goal checker yaw tolerance is 0.35 rad (20°), which is larger than the coast. The emulator therefore slows (|w| = clamp(|e|, 0.1, 0.5)) and stops on crossing.

## Residual risks
- MC "stall then release" with a wound-up u (up to 1.0) gives about 0.8 s at ~1.8 rad/s. In the sim that meant up to ~26° overshoot on a 90° turn. RPP re-commands, which could cost a correction or two.
- u_ff for CW above 0.6 is extrapolated (not measured). The integral corrects it.
- Final heading accuracy still depends on RPP and the goal checker. The adapter does not know the remaining angle; the coast is ~0.3 s of rate.
- Not verified live. No unit tests (operator rule).
