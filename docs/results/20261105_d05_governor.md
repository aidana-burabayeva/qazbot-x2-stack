## 1. Confirmed root cause (refined with the E2E-02 bag)
Chain, each link measured in `bag_e2e02_20261105_1707` (local; evidence copy `docs/evidence/e2e02_20261105/`):
1. MC walks ~0.50 m/s for forward cmd 0.20 (52 steady samples, sd 0.05; 0.30 -> 0.48 in D-05), dead time 0.4-0.5 s, coast after
   cmd->0: 0.07-0.31 m (8 stops, fit 0.38 s*v, residual sd 0.054 m; `test/data/e2e02_stop_events.txt`).
2. The robot arrives at the end of the path with a lateral error of 0.10-0.18 m (E2E-02 and SL0.20 runs, same sign) and a yaw
   error of -0.16..-0.27 rad. Realised curvature is wz/v_real = 0.4 of the planned one (D-05 overspeed), so RPP corrects slowly.
3. At +7.1 s the robot is 0.104 m from the goal (xy tolerance 0.10): RPP does NOT use rotate-to-goal-heading, it takes
   rotate-to-path (carrot behind, angle -1.56 rad) and turns around at -0.6 rad/s. 4 mm outside the disc decide the branch.
4. Turn-arounds shift the body randomly (mean speed 0.13 m/s in pure rotation, no preferred direction in the body frame:
   forward -0.04, lateral -0.01 m/s; LiDAR lever arm explains 6 % of it), so the next approach starts from a new error.
Along-track overshoot (0.19 m) is one factor; the lateral error against the 0.10 m disc and the RPP branch at the disc edge are the other.
Not the cause: adapter/MC chain (RPP=smoother=authorized, MC source sdk, FSM 4/5), yaw loop (active, 0 stale), LiDAR offset.

## 5. Capabilities and limits (honest)
- Removes the along-track overshoot caused by dead time + coast, in simulation by +5..+10 percentage points of success and ~10 % fewer
  turn-arounds. It does NOT remove the limit cycle: robot step quantum (~0.3 m per creep) and the lateral error (0.10-0.18 m against the
  0.10 m disc) remain. The simulation cannot prove behaviour on the robot (assumptions A1-A8 in the test header, one flight bag).
- Not changed on purpose: xy/yaw tolerance, RPP rotate_to_heading parameters, planner, AMCL, costmap, smoother.
- Longer stop_time (0.6 s) looked better in simulation because it stops short and creeps; not adopted (measured value 0.38-0.40 s).

Verifier findings (independent, `.orchestrator/reports/20261105-d05-governor-verify.md`, PASS for the offline scope, not for D-05 as a whole):
- the 8-stop replay is in-sample (stop_time fitted on the same stops; a 9th stop with a turn was excluded) - no held-out data;
- `PathProgress::remaining` uses the nearest segment: can jump on paths that double back (not the case for straight goals);
- governor as sole publisher on the Goal Coordinator topic: node death = silence; GC/adapter timeout behaviour with it is untested;
- odom stamp clock offset > 0.5 s makes the speed silently unknown (fail-open, no warning yet).
