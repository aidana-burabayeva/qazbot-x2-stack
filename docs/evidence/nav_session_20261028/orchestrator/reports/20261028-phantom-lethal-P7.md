## (3) Why the cells stay after ClearEntireCostmap
- The clear does work (cleared at 266, 365, 549; the replays show nothing near the robot after 266 and 549).
- After 365 the cells came back because the robot really turned about 190 deg (RC/manual) and saw real objects 1.0-1.5 m away. Once it turned back they were behind it.
- ObstacleLayer has no time decay, so marked cells only clear when a valid ray passes through them. Valid rays only exist inside about ±45 deg and only for finite beams (inf_is_valid=false). Out-of-FOV and inf-sector cells therefore persist as long as they are inside the 4x4 rolling window.
- This is by design under AGENTS.md: unknown space is not declared free.
