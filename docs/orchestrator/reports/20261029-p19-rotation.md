## Fix staged (adapter), NOT yet live
x2_motion_adapter.py adds a "turn-stall kick" (Config.turn_kick, ROS params turn_kick/turn_stall_window_s/turn_stall_min_rad/turn_kick_zero_s/odom_topic).
- It acts only on a pure in-place command (|vx|,|vy|<eps, |wz|>=eps) with fresh /qazbot_native/odom (age <=0.3 s).
- When yaw progress over 1.2 s is under 0.05 rad, it publishes ZERO for 0.8 s (> foundationwalk vel_yaw_cooldown_sec 0.6) and then passes the RPP command through unchanged. turn_kicks is counted in the status counters.
- It only inserts zeros and never raises or adds motion, so it cannot cause overshoot. RPP and the goal checker (0.35) still decide when to stop.
- Offline replay through AdapterCore on the bag (not a test suite): kicks fire in both stall windows (first one t+13.2 s / t+40.9 s, every ~2 s). There were no kicks in the working CW turn or in 1793268764.
- Efficacy is UNPROVEN live: no bag segment shows a restart from a plateau. The hypothesis rests only on "start from rest turns".
- py_compile OK. Backups *.bak_20261029_pre_p19 for the adapter (ws + qazbot-x2-stack), start_nav.sh and restart_gc.sh. Mirrored to qazbot-x2-stack.

  1. The current adapter has no DELETE-on-exit, so a restart needs a SetMcInputSource DELETE + ADD of sdk_qazbot_nav2_1029a. That is a separate, approved, robot-standing step.

## Conclusions (root cause)
1. **The MC/robot causes this, not the adapter or Nav2.**
   - Direct MC commands reproduce the deadband (CCW <=0.5 gives no sustained turn) at the same priority and rate.
   - The adapter forwards values unchanged (part 1 plus the bag: RPP = smoother = authorized = 0.6).
2. Sustained in-place CCW needs |w| >= 0.6 from standing, which is marginal (0.35-0.53 rad/s), and >= 0.8 to be reliable.
   - Above that MC overspeeds: commanded 0.8 gives 1.25 rad/s and 1.0 gives 1.87 rad/s.
   - After walking, 0.6 drops to 0.2-0.37 rad/s with 23-38% stall in both directions.
3. **Pelvis vs torso:** no large "pelvis first, then legs" decoupling was observed in chest/torso/pelvis yaw (waist <=9 deg).
   - Small reversals of 4-8 deg after stopping; up to 18 deg after the kick and at CCW 0.2.
   - The operator-observed back-rotation was NOT reproduced at this scale. Feet were not measured.
4. **The staged turn-stall kick (zero 0.8 s) is NOT supported by the data.** After walking it was no better than without it (0.34 vs 0.37 rad/s) and had the largest reversal. Do NOT deploy it. The adapter file is staged only; revert it with the .bak_20261029_pre_p19 backup.
5. **Candidate fix (needs design and approval):**
   - For pure in-place turns, command |w| >= 0.8 CCW, roughly 0.6-0.8 CW from stand and higher after walking. The adapter or RPP would do this.
   - Because MC then turns at ~1.25 rad/s, stop early with a lead of about rate x 0.3 s to avoid overshoot.
   - The other approach is to stop -> STAND before the final turn, since starts from stand are best.
   - Calibrate the overspeed and the coast before choosing.
