Findings:
- Rate 0.99-1.05 cm/min in EVERY standing period (total ~80 min of standing over 4 h), per-minute chunks 0.98-1.06 cm.
- Direction +88.3..+91.1 deg in BODY frame (pure body-LEFT) at headings 20, 133, 145, -33, -39, -216 deg => body-fixed, not world-fixed.
- Linear-fit residual 0.010-0.020 cm over up to 28 min => a CONSTANT VELOCITY (~1.7e-4 m/s lateral), not noise integration (a random
  walk would give a varying direction and sqrt(t) growth). z and yaw do not drift (yaw 0.000 deg over 14-28 min => the MC has some
  yaw hold / zero-rate handling while standing, but none for position).
- Dependence on MC state: NOT TESTABLE — all standing data is fsm_state 4, action STAND_DEFAULT, body_status 1, speed_mode 1, body_pose
  rc 0/0 (stand_height/bend 0). No dependence on input source (rc vs sdk). No dependence on heading.
- Dependence on IMU yaw: none observed (yaw constant; drift direction follows body).
- FAST-LIO in the same windows: no trend (0.03-0.35 cm/min net), bounded wander: std 0.25-0.63 cm (quiet scenes) up to 1.9-2.1 cm
  (d04 bags, cloud recording + people), max excursion 7 cm, step jitter p50 2-3 mm, p99 0.6-1.0 cm, max 1.3 cm.
- HYPOTHESIS for the vendor mechanism (cannot be verified, closed source): a lateral accelerometer bias / IMU roll misalignment
  (g*sin(roll)) balanced against the leg-kinematic velocity correction in the MC filter gives a steady-state velocity offset; or a
  constant lateral offset in the stance-leg kinematic velocity. Either way: deterministic, body-frame, constant.
- Symptom: costmap marks (fixed in odom) creep toward the robot's LEFT side at ~1 cm/min while it stands; 30 cm after 30 min.
  Proximate: odom->base_footprint moves body-left at ~1.7e-4 m/s while the robot is physically still.
  Root: vendor MC leg-odometry estimator velocity bias while standing; QazBot uses that estimate as the sole odom source with no
  standing/ZUPT handling (bridge is a pure pass-through by design).

### Is FAST-LIO a better odom source for the local costmap frame?
| criterion | native leg (MC) | FAST-LIO (level_bridge) |
|---|---|---|
| standing drift | 1.0 cm/min, unbounded, body-left | none (bounded wander 0.3-2 cm std, max 7 cm) |
| short-term smoothness | excellent (no jitter) | 2-3 mm p50, ~1 cm p99 step jitter |
| rate / latency (bag recv - stamp) | 139-170 Hz; p50 4.5-7 ms, p99 8-14 ms (one bag 180 ms) | 10 Hz; p50 32-40 ms, p99 44-94 ms, max 0.33-1.06 s |

| known failure modes | silent constant drift; origin reset (latched by JumpGuard) | in-place turn drift 1.77 m (trial06, bridge docstring); ~0.3 m/s phantom lateral velocity while standing (trial06, native_odom.py l.49-50); "lidar loop back, clear buffer" + "Too few input point cloud" twice on 11-02 (localization.log l.237, l.1261) with level_bridge "odometry stamp went back" and stamps at ~0.095 s => output gap/timestamp fault; degeneracy risk in feature-poor scenes |
Verdict: FAST-LIO is better for STANDING stability and walking scale, worse for latency and has rarer but larger, discontinuous failures
(turn drift, stamp resets) that would move all costmap marks at once. Switching odom source wholesale trades a known, small, constant
error
for rare large ones => NOT recommended as the first fix. Preferred: keep native as base, remove its standing drift (fix A below);
consider a LIO-checked hybrid only after the trial06 turn failure is re-qualified.

## 2. Q2 — how out-of-FOV (blind) marks survive (mechanisms)
Coverage facts (from 20261102-d04-P1P2 and d04b): marking span ~-58.8..+54.4 deg (raw sensor +-61), clearing FIXED [-46, +44.5] deg,
min mark range 0.45 m, raw min range 0.69 m; ObstacleLayer has no decay; only clearing rays, footprint clearing, rolling-window exit
or Clear*Costmap remove a LETHAL cell.
Ways a cell becomes uncleareable and survives:
 M1 Marked in a mark-only edge sector (+44.5..+54 / -46..-59 deg): never clearable from that pose (D-04 primary; transient people).
 M2 Rotation in place (Nav2 rotate-to-heading 0.6 rad/s, RC turns): an in-FOV mark of a real object is swept beyond +-46 deg before
    the object leaves; if the object then moves away the mark is stale and invisible to clearing.
 M3 Translation past objects: walking forward, side objects (walls, chairs, people) end up at |bearing| > 46 deg or inside 0.45 m;
    marks of real static objects are CORRECT memory here, but become stale if the object moves.
 M4 Relocation with the stack running (manual push/carry/RC to a new place): all marks are carried in odom; objects seen during the
    relocation path end up beside/behind the robot (d04b cluster at +70.9 deg/0.37 m: marked during unrecorded relocation 19:29-20:32).
 M5 Standing drift (Q1): moves every odom-fixed mark body-RIGHTwards relative to the robot... precisely: robot moves body-left in odom,
    so marks on the LEFT approach the left footprint edge (d04b: cluster grew into CCW-rotation and fwd-left collisions after 5 cm)
    and marks on the right edge move further right (deeper into the blind sector). Drift does not create marks; it transports them.
 M6 Odom discontinuity (LIO stamp reset/turn drift if LIO were the source; origin reset latched for native) — would shift all marks.
 M7 Footprint clearing only removes cells INSIDE the padded footprint; marks just outside the outline (d04b: 0.06 m) survive and
    block RPP projections.

## 3. Q3 — root-cause fixes (no decay, no automatic clear)
A. (RECOMMENDED, root of M5) Standing hold in native_odom_bridge: when MC reports standing (fsm_state 4 + STAND_DEFAULT + no MC
   locomotion command for >= T, and leg pose speed below the bias level), freeze the published x,y (hold the last pose, keep yaw from MC)
   and re-anchor on exit: publish p_hold + (p_leg - p_leg_at_hold_start) only for motion after the hold ends, i.e. accumulate a
   correction offset = bias integrated during the hold. Pure library change in native_odom.py (testable), node only subscribes
   /aima/mc/common/state. Removes ~1 cm/min; worst case if wrongly held during a slow real slide: bounded by the hold gate.
   Alternative A2: subtract a fitted body-frame lateral bias (1.7e-4 m/s) always — NOT recommended (vendor-dependent constant, unknown
   while walking, D-06 shows walking errors are speed-dependent).
B. (Optional, after re-qualifying trial06) LIO-gated standstill: use FAST-LIO only as a standstill detector (LIO 2 s speed < 1 cm/s)
   to confirm the hold, not as the pose source.
C. Do NOT switch the local costmap frame to FAST-LIO now (see table: latency, stamp resets, turn drift).
D. Coverage (root of M1, from P1P2): align clearing FOV with the marking span (or crop marking to the clearing FOV with operator
   acceptance) — separate decision, outside this task.
E. Operator procedure for relocations (root of M4), below.

### Operator checklist — manual relocation (no automatic clear)
1. Before moving: cancel any active goal; confirm robot is in STAND_DEFAULT and Nav2 is idle.
2. Preferred: stop the Nav2 control stack (controller/planner/costmaps) before relocating; localization may stay up.
   If the stack must stay up: accept that marks will be carried; continue with step 5.
3. Relocate (RC/push). Keep people away from the robot's sides during the move where possible.
4. At the new place: set the initial pose (RViz /initialpose) and verify AMCL/trust is OK.
5. Look at the robot physically AND at the local costmap in RViz: identify LETHAL cells beside/behind the robot (outside the
   +-45 deg front sector) and check by eye that the corresponding space is free.
6. Only if the space is visually free: trigger local ClearEntireCostmap (operator action, nav2_msgs/srv/ClearEntireCostmap on
   .../local_costmap/clear_entirely_local_costmap); if anything is physically there, do NOT clear — move the object or the robot.
7. After clearing: watch 2-3 s that the front sector re-marks real obstacles; confirm the costmap now matches what you see.
8. Restart the stack (if stopped) and only then send a goal. If the robot stood > 10 min before the goal, repeat steps 5-7
   (standing drift 1 cm/min moves left-side marks toward the footprint until fix A is deployed).
9. Log the relocation time and the clear in operator_actions.txt.
